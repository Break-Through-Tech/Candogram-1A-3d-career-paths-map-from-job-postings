About

With our 3D Career Map: Explore possible career paths from entry-level to senior roles using job postings.

The dataset used for this project is current job postings available on the City of New York’s official jobs site provided by the Department of Citywide Administrative Services (DCAS).

Features

The interactive view shows a 3D UMAP projection of job titles, colored by career level and enriched with hover details.

How to Navigate

Use the mouse to rotate the plot, zoom in for larger clusters, and hover over points to view job titles and career-level metadata.
